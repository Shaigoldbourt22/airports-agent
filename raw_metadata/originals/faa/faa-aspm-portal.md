## DJT data is now available from 8/11/2026 forward in ASPM. Data from 7/9/2026-8/10/2026 will be available after the next ASQP load.
  
  

* ASPM: Airport Analysis, City Pair Analysis and Taxi Time data will be available within 60 days after the end of the month; i.e., August data will be available no later than November 1. 
* ATADS: Data previously available in ATADS is now available in OPSNET. 
* OPSNET: Operations and Delay data for each month will be available on the 20th of the next month; i.e., August data will be available on September 20. 
* TFMSC: Airport and Distributed OPSNET data for each month will be available within 31 days after the end of the month; i.e., September data will be available no later than November 1. 
* ASQP: Operations by Airport data will be available within 60 days after the end of the month; i.e., August data will be available no later than November 1.   
  
An account is required to access non‑public **www.aspm.faa.gov** reports that contain preliminary data and individual flight information. If you do not already have an ASPM account, please sign in using the [_**MyAccess Login**_] button at the top right of the screen and choose one of the available MyAccess authentication options. FAA employees and contractors will automatically receive enhanced access privileges upon logging in. All other users may request elevated permissions by selecting their name in the top right corner and following the [_**Request elevated access privileges**_] link beneath their profile details.
