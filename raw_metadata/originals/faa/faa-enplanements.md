# Passenger Boarding (Enplanement) and All-Cargo Data for U.S. Airports
Passenger boarding (enplanement) and all-cargo data is extracted from the FAA's Air Carrier Activity database. This database supports the FAA's airport categories for use in apportionment formula calculations under the [Airport Improvement Program (AIP)](/airports/aip) and [Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act programs](/iija).  
[More about the collection and use of passenger and all-cargo data »](/airports/planning_capacity/passenger_allcargo_stats/passenger/collection)
Passenger boarding and all-cargo data is collected for a full calendar year and is used in calculating airport categories and entitlements for the next full fiscal year (i.e., Calendar Year 2025 data determines Fiscal Year 2027 category and funds).
  * **Preliminary** Calendar Year 2025 enplanement and all-cargo data will be available in June 2026.
    * Any major discrepancies or have questions concerning preliminary all-cargo data or enplanements should promptly be directed to Mr. Luis Loarte at [Luis.Loarte@faa.gov](mailto:Luis.Loarte@faa.gov?subject=Question%20about%20all-cargo%20data%20and%20enplanements) or Mrs. Christina Nutting at [Christina.Nutting@faa.gov](mailto:Christina.Nutting@faa.gov?subject=Question%20about%20all-cargo%20data%20and%20enplanements) by August 14, 2026.
  * **Final** Calendar Year 2025 enplanement and all-cargo data will become available in late August 2026.


Airports can view their individual CY 2025 enplanement report on our [Airports External Portal (AEP)](https://aep.airports.faa.gov/Home.aspx). Please select "Enplanement Public Report and Form" on the AEP site.
See Abbreviations for an explanation of the abbreviations used in the enplanements and cargo files column headings.

## Abbreviations
  * CY = Calendar Year (January 1 through December 31)
  * RO = [FAA Regional Office](/about/office_org/headquarters_offices/arp/offices/regional_offices)
  * ST = Abbreviation for U.S. State or Territory
  * Locid = Location Identifier for a specific airport
  * S/L = Service Level - These are [Airport Categories](/airports/planning_capacity/categories) (P = Primary, CS = Commercial Service, GA = General Aviation).
  * Hub = Hub Size and applies to Commercial Service [Airport Categories](/airports/planning_capacity/categories) (L = Large, M = Medium, S = Small, N = Nonhub Primary, and None = All airports but Primary) based on total CY enplanements.
